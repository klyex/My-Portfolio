With the help of this, users will effortlessly use this method by browsing, exploring trailers, to select and secure their preferred seats with a few clicks, all from the comfort of their own devices. PyroShield: IoT-Based RFID Access Control with Automated Fire Alert System

PyroShield also includes an IoT-based fire detection module that relies on a heat sensor and temperature sensor to monitor ambient temperature levels. The sensor continuously observes environmental conditions and triggers alerts when abnormal temperature readings are detected, ensuring faster responses during fire-related incidents.
